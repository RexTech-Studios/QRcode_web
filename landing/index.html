How It Works

Turn Customer Feedback into More Google Reviews

A simple QR solution for your shop that collects customer feedback and makes it easy for customers to share their experience.

  1. 1

    Scan the QR Code

    Customers scan your shop's unique QR code using their smartphone camera.

  2. 2
    Your Feedback
    Share your feedback...
    Submit

    Give a Rating & Feedback

    Customers rate your service from 1 to 5 stars and can submit their feedback.

  3. 3
    1 – 3 Stars

    Feedback is saved in your private Google Sheet, visible only to the shop owner.

    4 – 5 Stars

    Customers are provided with an option to visit Google Maps and share their public review.

    Smart & Automatic

    Feedback is collected automatically, helping shop owners understand customer experiences and improve their services.
